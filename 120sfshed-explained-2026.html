Do You Need a Permit for a Shed?

Permit requirements for sheds vary by municipality, but the general rule is: structures under 120 square feet often do not require a building permit in most US jurisdictions. This is why 10x12 and 12x10 shed sizes are so popular — they sit just under the threshold. However, this threshold varies: some jurisdictions allow up to 200 sq ft without a permit, others require permits for any permanent structure regardless of size. Always check your local zoning ordinance before building.

Even when a building permit is not required, other rules may apply. Setback requirements (minimum distance from property lines, fences, and the main structure) apply in almost every jurisdiction. HOA rules may be more restrictive than municipal zoning. Some areas require permits for any structure with a concrete foundation, even small ones. The safest approach is to call your local building department — most will tell you the rules over the phone in 10 minutes.
